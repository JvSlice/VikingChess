How to play

Dark attackers move first. Tap one of your pieces, then a highlighted square. Pieces slide any distance in a straight line; they cannot jump over pieces.

Attackers win by capturing the king or surrounding every defender. The light side wins when the king reaches a corner. Other pieces are captured by sandwiching them between enemies or a hostile refuge square.

The king alone may stop on the center throne or a corner. The throne is passable when empty. Three repeated positions are a draw.

Two players · one device
